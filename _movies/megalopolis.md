It was wonderful seeing Megalopolis at the Palace of Fine Arts and to have Francis Ford Coppola come on stage for a Q and A. That being said, the movie itself is one of the worst I've seen... The effects, plot, and acting all failed in my opinion and it is clear that this film was a passion project limited by funds. However, the movie was still an enjoyable watch and I believe that it will atract a cult following similar to The Room one day.
